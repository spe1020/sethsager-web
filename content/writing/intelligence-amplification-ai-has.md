The most important technology of our generation has a branding problem. Artificial intelligence. Say “artificial” out loud. Artificial plants. Artificial sweeteners. Artificial light. Almost every time we use that word, it’s about a substitute. Something standing in for the real thing. No wonder people feel uneasy about AI. Even the name sets the wrong frame. A better description is Intelligence Amplification. I first heard that framing from Brian Roemmele and it clicked instantly. It also reminded me of something Scott Adams has talked about for years: reframing changes everything. 

**An Amplifier Doesn’t Replace the Guitarist**
An amplifier doesn’t replace the guitarist. It extends the music’s reach. That’s exactly how these tools should work. Not as a substitute for our thinking, but as a way to strengthen it. That shift matters in the kitchen. When I’m standing in front of the fridge after a long day, I don’t want software pretending it’s cooking for me. I want off-ramp off ramps for the blank page, a steady hand on the choices so I don’t default to takeout again. Show me a couple directions that based on what I’ve got. Save me 20 minutes of dithering. Help me waste less food. I’m still the cook. It just amplifies my instincts instead of replacing them. I think this applies far beyond dinner.

**Technology Should Make Us More Human, Not Less**
The best technology won’t be the loudest. It’ll fade quietly into the background, making us a little more capable, a little more creative, a little more connected to the things we want to do. At Zap Cooking, that’s the North Star. Not software that replaces people. Tools that make people feel more confident, more creative, and more connected around food. If we’ve done our job well, you won’t remember the tech. You’ll remember the meal you shared with your family. Words matter because they shape expectations.

**Maybe We Don’t Need Better AI. We Need a Better Frame.**
So maybe what we need isn’t a new breakthrough. Maybe we just need a better name. Not artificial intelligence. Intelligence Amplification.
